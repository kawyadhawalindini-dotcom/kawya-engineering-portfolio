PROJECT 02 · RENEWABLE ENERGY

PV + Battery Energy Storage System

MATLAB/Simulink-based modelling of solar photovoltaic generation, battery energy storage, and intelligent energy management.

Back to Projects

01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

This project focuses on modelling a solar PV system integrated with a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS). The model will be used to investigate solar power generation, battery charging and discharging, and energy flow under changing operating conditions.

02 / OBJECTIVES

Project Objectives

PV Generation

Model solar photovoltaic output under different irradiance conditions.

Maximum Power Point Tracking

Investigate MPPT control to extract available power from the PV array.

Battery Management

Examine battery charging, discharging and state-of-charge behaviour.

Energy Management

Analyse power sharing between PV generation, battery storage and the connected load.

03 / SYSTEM MODEL

Proposed System Configuration

The planned model includes the following main subsystems. The final configuration will depend on the implemented MATLAB/Simulink model.

Solar PV Array

Converts solar irradiance into electrical power.

DC–DC Converter and MPPT

Regulates PV operating conditions to track the maximum power point.

Battery Energy Storage

Stores excess energy and supplies energy when required.

Load and Energy Management

Represents demand and the control of energy flow within the system.

04 / METHODOLOGY

Simulation Methodology

  1. Develop the PV array model in MATLAB/Simulink.
  2. Implement the MPPT controller and DC–DC converter.
  3. Integrate the battery model and monitor its state of charge.
  4. Connect the load and define energy management conditions.
  5. Simulate different irradiance and load scenarios.
  6. Record and analyse voltage, current, power and battery SOC.

05 / RESULTS

Simulation Results

Simulation results will be added after the model is implemented and tested. No numerical results are claimed at this stage.

Planned Performance Analysis

  • PV voltage, current and output power.
  • MPPT response under changing irradiance.
  • Battery state of charge during charging and discharging.
  • Power balance between PV generation, battery and load.
